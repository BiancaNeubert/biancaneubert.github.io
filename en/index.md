Since September 2026, I am a post-doc at MAP5 and LPSM on the topic “Non-parametric statistics in the functional convolution model with application to PCA,” under the supervision of <a href="https://aroche01.pages.math.cnrs.fr/index/" target="_blank">Angelina Roche</a> (MAP5) and <a href="https://www.lpsm.paris/users/fischer/fischer_en" target="_blank">Aurélie Fischer</a> (LPSM)  with the <a href="https://complexcite.u-pariscite.fr/postdoc/" target="_blank">ComplexCité 2026 postdoctoral fellowship</a> of Université Paris Cité.

I defended my PhD thesis entitled  <a href="https://archiv.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/volltextserver/37748/" target="_blank">"Nonparametric inference in convolution models"</a> supervised by <a href="https://www.janjohannes.de" target="_blank">Jan Johannes</a> and <a href="https://helios2.mi.parisdescartes.fr/~comte/" target="_blank">Fabienne Comte</a> on the 4th December 2025 in Heidelberg.

My reasearch intersts include:
<ul>
  <li>Nonparametric estimation and hypothesis testing</li>
  <li>Additive and multiplicative (de-)convolution models</li>
  <li>Funcitonal data analysis</li>
</ul>
